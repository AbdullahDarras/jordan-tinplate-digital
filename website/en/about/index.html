Our story

A manufacturer that grew with its customers

Jordan Tinplate Canning has grown into a manufacturer with fully automatic production lines, in-house design and skilled people. We make tinplate cans and tins for food and beverage brands, and handle the journey from artwork to export-ready packs.

Vision & mission

Where we are going

Our vision: to lead metal packaging in the region and be the first choice for customers around the globe.

Our mission is to deliver high-end quality products and services using cutting-edge technology and a skilled workforce.
