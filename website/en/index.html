Our story

Metal, made by one family, since 1998.

28years of making metal packaging

Founded in 1998 and led today by the third generation of the family, Jordan Tinplate Canning has grown into a manufacturer with fully automatic production lines, in-house design and skilled people.

Our vision: to lead metal packaging in the region and be the first choice for customers around the globe.

Our mission is to deliver high-end quality products and services using cutting-edge technology and a skilled workforce.
