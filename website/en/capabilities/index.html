Step by step

From sheet to shelf.

  1. Design & plates

    In-house designers prepare your artwork, and CTP (Computer-to-Plate) makes every printing plate precise.

  2. Print, coat & finish

    High-end inks and coatings, plus effects: gloss, matte, soft touch, rough touch and embossing.

  3. Cut & form

    Sheets are cut on exact lines and formed into your shape: round, square, rectangular or custom.

  4. Pack & palletise

    Cartons are palletised with methods tailored to each client and suited to overseas shipping.

  5. Store & ship

    We can hold finished stock for you, freeing your own storage, then ship it to your markets.

Printing & finishes

Touch it before you print it.

High-end inks and coatings, plus effects. Every finish below is available on our lines.

  • Gloss

    Special gloss, alone or combined with matte, for bright, lively visuals on the metal.

  • Matte

    A low-glare surface that gives colours a calmer, deeper look.

  • Soft touch

    A silky coating over the whole pack that invites touch.

  • Rough touch

    A textured coating that stimulates the senses and gives the pack a distinctive look.

  • Embossing

    Raised and recessed effects on lid and body that add movement and make the design stand out.
