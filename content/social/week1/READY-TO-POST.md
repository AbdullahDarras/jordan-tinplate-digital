## Notes on the designs / ملاحظات على التصاميم

The owner asked for these to be shown as an indirect portfolio (`brand/README.md`, rule 6). **Confirm the clients are happy to appear in social posts before publishing.**
- The logo is the supplied vector logo (reads "JORDAN CANNING", used as supplied per decision 36); every text keeps the full official name.
- No certificate, certificate logo, capacity figure, client name in the copy, or Saudi reference appears in any design.
- Sources are in `src/` (HTML + CSS). Change a word there and run `node content/social/week1/render.mjs` to export again; the reel is exported with `node content/social/week1/render-reel.mjs`. New tin renders come from the website engine (headless Chromium capture of the stages).
- The interactive version of this file, with every image, the reel and copy buttons, is `index.html` in this folder.
