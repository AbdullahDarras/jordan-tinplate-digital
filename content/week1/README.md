## Conventions

- `[تحقق]` (Arabic) and `[TBC]` (English) mark anything not confirmed. They stay in the files so nobody publishes an unconfirmed statement by accident; remove them only after confirmation. The olive-oil size and interior-lacquer statements are now confirmed and carry no marker; the light, toughness, stacking and finishes-on-oil-tins statements keep theirs, with fallbacks.
- `[مؤقت]` marks the provisional Arabic brand name «جوردن تينبليت كانينغ». Delete the marker and replace the name in all Arabic copy once the official Arabic name arrives; never publish the marker.
- Hashtag for the brand is always the full `#JordanTinplateCanning`. Handles: `@jordantinplatecanning` on Instagram/Facebook, `@jordantinplate` on X (X caps usernames at 15 characters), subject to availability.
- Arabic is written in clear Modern Standard Arabic that reads naturally for Jordan and the Gulf, not translated word by word. Dialect words are avoided except where they are the normal trade term ("تنكة").
- Numerals are Western (1 to 6, 25/10) as in the plan. No emojis in the copy, by design.
- Sample artwork only on any tin shown. No real client brand.
