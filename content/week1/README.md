## Conventions

- `[تحقق]` (Arabic) and `[TBC]` (English) mark anything not confirmed. They stay in the files so nobody publishes an unconfirmed statement by accident; remove them only after confirmation. Management approved keeping the markers on the light, toughness and stacking sentences until the technical body reviews them (round 3, item 24). The olive-oil size and interior-lacquer statements are now confirmed and carry no marker; the light, toughness and stacking statements keep theirs, with fallbacks.
- The official Arabic name «الشركة الأردنية لصناعة وطباعة الصفيح والعبوات المعدنية» is written in full in all Arabic copy. There is no provisional marker any more.
- Hashtag for the brand is always the full `#JordanTinplateCanning`. Username: `@jordantinplate` on every platform (decision, round 2), subject to availability. The profile display name is the official name (English: Jordan Tinplate Canning; Arabic: «الشركة الأردنية لصناعة وطباعة الصفيح والعبوات المعدنية», 54 characters, so it does not fit the Instagram name field (30) or the Facebook intro (101): the English name stays there and the Arabic name goes in the bio/About); only the handle is short.
- Arabic is written in clear Modern Standard Arabic that reads naturally for Jordan and the Gulf, not translated word by word. Dialect words are avoided except where they are the normal trade term ("تنكة").
- Numerals are Western (1 to 6, 25/10) as in the plan. No emojis in the copy, by design.
- Sample artwork only on any tin shown. No real client brand.
