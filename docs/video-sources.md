## 3) Rules for every clip (from the existing plans)

1. Mute the clip's own audio unless the team confirms the speech and music rights; use an instrumental track.
2. Crop or blur any client brand, logo or certificate that appears.
3. No on-screen text implying speed, capacity, "fully automatic" lines or a named standard.
4. No Saudi reference in any shot (no Saudi branch or office exists).
5. People who are clearly visible must have agreed to appear.
6. Use different clips for the reel (#4), X-E03, X-E09 and IG #12, so the same shot is not repeated in a week.
